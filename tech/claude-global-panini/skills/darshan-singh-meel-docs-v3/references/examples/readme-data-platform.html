Billing analytics platform

dbt + Airflow · nightly 02:00 UTC · app and billing databases → MRR board

  • Loads accounts and invoices every night
  • Builds monthly recurring revenue (MRR) per account in fct_mrr
  • Keeps account history in accounts_snap
  • Feeds the MRR board
  • Doesn't bill customers: the billing system owns invoices
  • Doesn't forecast revenue
  • MRR is final at 06:00 UTC; earlier reads show the previous day
  1. No known issues
