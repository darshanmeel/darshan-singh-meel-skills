# Subject rules: neutrality, data, material, judgement

## Beutelsbacher Konsens (1976)
| Principle | Key sentence (bpb) | In material |
|---|---|---|
| Überwältigungsverbot | "Es ist nicht erlaubt, den Schüler - mit welchen Mitteln auch immer - im Sinne erwünschter Meinungen zu überrumpeln" | no loaded wording, no single-sided material set, no "right" opinion in the solution |
| Kontroversitätsgebot | "Was in Wissenschaft und Politik kontrovers ist, muss auch im Unterricht kontrovers erscheinen." | at least two positions with their strongest arguments; equal length, equal quality of source |
| Schülerorientierung (Interessenlage) | "Der Schüler muss in die Lage versetzt werden, eine politische Situation und seine eigene Interessenlage zu analysieren" | one task asks what the question means for the students and what they could do |

- The KCGO names the Kontroversitätsgebot as binding for the subject (KCGO section 2.1).
- Limits: positions against human dignity and the free democratic basic order (Art. 1, 20 GG) are not presented as one legitimate option among others; they are analysed (KC: "demokratiefeindliche Verhaltensweisen" are an object of critical study; standard H8).
- Teachers' duty of restraint in class: check: Hessisches Schulgesetz § 86 (wording not read).

### Neutrality test (writer and reviewer, every file)
1. Swap test: would the wording be accepted by supporters of each position? Replace evaluative words (`endlich`, `leider`, `nur`, `sogenannt`, `Chaos`, `Reform` as praise) by descriptive ones.
2. Balance: positions A and B have the same number of arguments, the same space, sources of the same weight.
3. Parties and persons: named only where the task needs them; then all relevant ones by the same rule (e.g. all parties in the parliament, in order of seats), never a selection.
4. Solution: marks reward criteria, evidence and weighing; the model answer shows two possible judgements.
5. Models and schools of thought (Keynesian vs. neoclassical, supply vs. demand side): each with assumptions, strengths, criticism.
6. Examples and pictures: no stereotype by origin, gender, religion, income.

## Real data
- Label under each chart or table: `Quelle: <institution>, <publication / table code>, Stand: <date of the data>, abgerufen: <date>`; English line: `Source: ..., as of: ..., retrieved: ...`.
- Use the newest release available on the day of writing; say in the reply which release was used. Data older than 2 years: say so, or replace.
- Give the unit, the reference area and the reference period in the table head (`in %`, `Deutschland`, `2. Quartal 2026`). Never round differently from the source.
- Time series: same definition across the years (note breaks). Percent vs. percentage points kept apart.
- Without network permission: ask the user for the figures, or build the task on labelled model figures.

## Material types
| Material | Rule for writing | Task pattern (operator, AFB) |
|---|---|---|
| Sachtext / position text | written for the task, 120-350 words, numbered lines, author role invented and labelled (`Text für diese Aufgabe verfasst`) | zusammenfassen (I) → herausarbeiten (II) → beurteilen (III) |
| Statistik, Diagramm | title, unit, source line; zero-based axes for bars; at most 2 series in Sek I | beschreiben (I) → auswerten / erklären (II) → beurteilen der Aussagekraft (III) |
| Karikatur | describe an invented cartoon in 3-5 sentences, or draw a simple original SVG; never copy a published cartoon | beschreiben (I) → analysieren / interpretieren (II-III) → Stellung nehmen (III) |
| Rechtstext | article + law + version date; quote only the paragraph needed | wiedergeben (I) → anwenden auf den Fall (II) |
| Fall (case) | invented persons, firm, town, marked invented; contains a conflict with at least two interests | see case study |
| Modell, Schaubild | own drawing; assumptions listed | beschreiben (I) → erklären / anwenden (II) → überprüfen der Grenzen (III) |

## Case study (Fallanalyse)
1. Konfrontation: what happened, who is involved (Akteure).
2. Analyse: interests, power, legal frame, causes (Basiskonzepte: structure, actors, process; Oberstufe: + Wandel).
3. Lösungsmöglichkeiten: at least two, each with consequences for each actor.
4. Urteil: criteria named → Sachurteil → Werturteil.
5. Generalisierung: what the case shows about the general problem; what it does not show.

- A case is small enough to hold in 150-250 words and open enough for two solutions.
- Real current cases: only from dated official sources; say the Stand; add "Die Lage kann sich geändert haben".

## Judgement tasks
| Step | Content | In the Erwartungshorizont |
|---|---|---|
| Kriterien | e.g. Effizienz, Wirksamkeit, Gerechtigkeit, Legitimität, Rechtmäßigkeit, Nachhaltigkeit, Freiheit, Sicherheit, Kosten | points for naming and using criteria |
| Sachurteil | does the measure reach the goal, with which side effects, for whom | points for evidence from material and course knowledge |
| Werturteil | weighing by values, own position | points for coherent weighing; both directions earn full marks |

- Operators: beurteilen = Sachurteil with criteria; bewerten / Stellung nehmen = adds own value standards; erörtern / diskutieren = pro and contra, then a reasoned result (Landesabitur list - read the definitions in the PDF).
- Macro methods (M2): Pro-Kontra-Debatte, Planspiel, Rollenspiel, Talkshow, Zukunftswerkstatt, Expertenbefragung - role cards are balanced and invented.

## Typical mistakes of students (use in "Typischer Fehler")
| Topic | Mistake | Right |
|---|---|---|
| Market | "Nachfrage" used for the quantity bought at one price | Nachfrage = the whole relation price → quantity; "nachgefragte Menge" at one price |
| Market | price change shifts the curve | a price change moves along the curve; other factors (income, tastes, costs) shift it |
| Diagram | price on the x-axis | convention: Preis on the y-axis, Menge on the x-axis |
| BIP | BIP = Wohlstand | BIP measures market production in a period; says nothing about distribution, unpaid work, environment |
| Inflation | falling inflation rate = falling prices | prices still rise, more slowly; falling prices = Deflation |
| Percent | change from 2 % to 3 % = "1 %" | 1 percentage point; 50 % relative |
| State | Bundestag "regiert" | Bundestag = Legislative; Bundesregierung = Exekutive |
| Elections | Erststimme more important than Zweitstimme | check the electoral law in force before stating how seats follow from the votes |
| EU | "Europarat" = "Europäischer Rat" = "Rat der EU" | three different bodies; the Europarat is not an EU institution |
| Judgement | opinion without criteria | criteria → evidence → weighing → result |

## Facts that change - verify or keep structural
Electoral law details, number of seats, office holders, coalition, party programmes, tax rates, Mindestlohn, Bürgergeld/Regelsätze, key interest rates, EU membership and candidates, treaty articles, court rulings. Write `Stand: <date>` or avoid the detail.
