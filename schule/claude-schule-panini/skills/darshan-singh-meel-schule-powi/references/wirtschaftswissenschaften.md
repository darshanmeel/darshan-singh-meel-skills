## Subject notes
- More calculation than in Politik und Wirtschaft: costs, productivity, elasticity, tax tariffs, exchange rates, comparative advantage. Every calculation task gives units and rounding; the solution shows each step.
- Party tax positions (Q1.5) and country data (Q4.1) are time-bound: dated official source or structural task (`fachregeln.md`).
- Which schools offer the subject, and as GK or LK: school decision; ask.
