# Grammar, spelling, language reflection

KC anchors: Inhaltsfelder Sprachformen und -strukturen, Analyseverfahren, Sprachnormen, Schreibnormen (`grundschule.md`, `sek1.md`); Oberstufe: Kompetenzbereich Sprache und Sprachgebrauch reflektieren (Sr), Themenfelder E1.1, E1.5, Q1.2, Q2.1, Q3.4 (`oberstufe.md`).

## Tests (Proben) - the proof behind every grammar answer
| Probe | What is done | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| Umstellprobe (Verschiebeprobe) | move word groups; in a statement the finite verb stays in second place | what moves together is one Satzglied |
| Ersatzprobe | replace a word or group by another (pronoun, question word) | Satzglied borders; case (`wen oder was?`); das/dass (`dieses, welches`) |
| Weglassprobe | leave out step by step | what the sentence needs (Subjekt, Prädikat, required Objekte) |
| Erweiterungsprobe | add words | Attribute, adverbiale Bestimmungen; makes texts more exact |
| Klangprobe | read aloud | sentence borders, style |
| Frageprobe | `Wer oder was?` `Wen oder was?` `Wem?` `Wessen?` `Wo? Wann? Wie? Warum?` | which Satzglied |
| Artikelprobe, Mehrzahlprobe | put an article in front; form the plural | Nomen → capital letter |
| Steigerungsprobe | schnell - schneller - am schnellsten | Adjektiv |
| Personalformprobe | ich laufe, du läufst | Verb |

The Lösung names the Probe and shows it once (`Am Morgen | holt | der kleine Hund | die Zeitung.`).

## Spelling strategies
| Strategy (KC term) | Children's name (common, ask the school) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| alphabetisch / Lautprinzip | Mitsprechen, Silben schwingen | Ba-na-ne |
| morphematisch / Stammprinzip | Verlängern, Ableiten, Wortfamilie | Hund - Hunde · Bäume - Baum · fahren - Fahrt |
| orthographisch | Merkwörter, Regel nachdenken | Vater, Fahrrad, ihr |
| grammatisches Prinzip | Großschreibung, das/dass, Komma | das Laufen · ich weiß, dass … |
| Hilfen | Wörterbuch, Wortliste, Rechtschreibgespräch | - |

- Norm: amtliches Regelwerk der deutschen Rechtschreibung (Rat für deutsche Rechtschreibung). check: current edition of the Regelwerk on rechtschreibrat.com before teaching a disputed case.
- Each spelling item in a task has exactly one correct form under the Regelwerk; avoid words with allowed variants (`Delfin/Delphin`, `selbstständig/selbständig`) in tests.
- Error texts (find the mistakes): at most 5 marked places in Grundschule, 8-10 in Sek I; only practised phenomena; the corrected text is in the Lösung in full.

- Konjunktion (Bindewort) comes in as a working term with Satzreihe and Satzgefüge in Jg. 5/6 (`und`, `weil`, `dass`); as a Wortart with its subclasses it is a focus of Jg. 7/8. Naming it in Jg. 5/6 is no level finding; classifying Nebensatzarten there is.

Zeitformen named by the school's term set: Präsens/Gegenwart, Präteritum/1. Vergangenheit, Perfekt/2. Vergangenheit, Plusquamperfekt, Futur I, Futur II.

## Building language tasks
- Material first: sentences or a short text written for the task; every item tested alone for one clear answer.
- Avoid traps unless they are the lesson: Verbklammer (`Er ruft seine Oma an.` - Prädikat has two parts), Prädikativ (`Sie ist Lehrerin.`), Präpositionalobjekt, `es` as placeholder, zu-Infinitiv, substantivierte Verben in Grundschule items on Wortarten.
- Umstellprobe items: statement sentences with 3-5 Satzglieder; the finite verb second; no separable verb in the first items.
- Wortarten items: words inside sentences, not lists - `laut` (Adjektiv) vs. `der Laut` (Nomen).
- Language reflection in the Oberstufe: always tied to a text or utterance (Sr2); models named with author (de Saussure, Bühler, Schulz von Thun, Watzlawick; Koch/Oesterreicher for Nähe und Distanz) and applied, not recited.

## Typical mistakes of the task writer
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| sentence allows two analyses | replace the sentence |
| solution uses a term the class has not had | take the term set of the level table |
| spelling item with an allowed variant | choose another word |
| Lückentext with more than one fitting word | add a Wortspeicher or accept both in the Lösung |
| English help line translates the example sentence | delete it; `data-nur-de` |
| dictation contains unpractised phenomena | swap the words; state the practised rules in the header |
