## Klassenarbeiten Deutsch
- Number per year, announcement, repetition: base `bewertung.md`.
- Jg. 5-6: at least half of the Klassenarbeiten train spelling (for example Diktat); the others are connected texts written by the student (VOGSV Anlage 2 Nr. 7.2, read in the base).
- Jg. 9/10: Fehlerindex in all subjects, in force since 1 August 2023, for written work with at least 100 words of connected text; deduction up to two thirds of a grade (Maßnahmenpaket item 6). check: VOGSV Anlage 2 Nr. 2.3 for thresholds and steps before printing them.
- At least one Ganzschrift per school year in Deutsch (Maßnahmenpaket item 7, binding since 2022/23): ask which one the class reads before proposing texts.
- Corrected spelling (correct form added) is binding up to Jg. 6 (Maßnahmenpaket item 5).
- Length: school decision (base). State minutes in the header.

| Form | Parts | Points default |
|---|---|---|
| Diktat + Arbeitsteil | text of practised words and rules (Jg. 5: 120-150 words, Jg. 6: 150-180; own estimate, Fachkonferenz decides) + 3-4 rule tasks | error key from the Fachkonferenz - ask; never invent one for grading |
| Grammatikarbeit | text-based tasks: bestimmen, umformen, einsetzen, begründen | points per item; AFB I-II, one AFB III task |
| Aufsatz | one writing task with material, form and scope named | Inhalt + Darstellung grid: `schreibaufgaben.md` |
| Lesen / Textarbeit | text + closed and half-open tasks + one short written answer | points per item; answers with line references |

## Language level of the material
- Jg. 5-6: sentences up to about 15 words, du-form, operators from the base Sek I list plus the Deutsch work verbs.
- Jg. 7-10: Oberstufe operators in du-form, introduced one by one with a model answer.
- Texts: Jg. 5-6 up to about 350 words; Jg. 7-8 up to 500; Jg. 9-10 up to 700 for a 45-minute lesson (own estimate).

## Typical mistakes to build into Lösung and Tipps
| Topic | Mistake | Proof or rule to teach |
|---|---|---|
| Satzglieder | Präpositionalobjekt vs. adverbiale Bestimmung | Frageprobe: Worauf? (Objekt, Präposition fest am Verb) vs. Wo? Wann? Wie? Warum? |
| Attribut | counted as its own Satzglied | Umstellprobe: the Attribut moves with its noun |
| das/dass | dass for the article or pronoun | Ersatzprobe: dieses, jenes, welches fits → das |
| Komma | missing before Nebensatz, between Hauptsätze without und | finite verb at the end marks the Nebensatz |
| Inhaltsangabe | Präteritum, direct speech, own opinion | Präsens, indirekte Rede, sachlich |
| Indirekte Rede | Indikativ kept | Konjunktiv I; Konjunktiv II or würde when the form equals the Indikativ |
| Erörterung | assertion without Begründung and Beispiel | These → Argument → Beleg/Beispiel (3-B-Schema) |
| Interpretation | retelling instead of analysing; Stilmittel named without effect | Behauptung → Beleg (Zitat, Zeile) → Deutung |
| Zitieren | quotation without line, changed wording, not built into the sentence | `texte.md` |
