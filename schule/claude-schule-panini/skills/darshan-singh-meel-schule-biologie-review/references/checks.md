- Explanations and assessments: list the expected key points first, then compare; a missing key point in the Erwartungshorizont is a finding.
- Figures: describe from the figure alone what it shows, then compare with the caption and the solution.
- Data tasks: read at least three values from the diagram and compare them with the table and the solution text.

## Frequent defects (look for these first)
- Chromatids counted as chromosomes; "46 Chromosomen" turned into "92 Chromosomen" in the metaphase; daughter cells of mitosis called haploid.
- Phase figure shows the nuclear envelope in the metaphase, or spindle fibres that end nowhere.
- Meiosis: homologues separated in the second division; four cells with 2n.
- Crossing scheme: gametes with two alleles of one gene; phenotype ratio given as genotype ratio.
- Pedigree fits both autosomal recessive and X-linked recessive, but the solution claims proof.
- Osmosis explained as "Salz zieht Wasser an" or as movement of salt; water said to move "to the lower concentration" without naming of what.
- Enzymes "die" at low temperature; optimum curves symmetrical for temperature.
- Photosynthesis equation unbalanced or without light; "Pflanzen atmen nachts".
- Action potential height grows with stimulus strength; refractory period missing where a task needs it.
- Evolution: "die Tiere passen sich an, um zu überleben" in the model answer.
- Energy "circulates" in the ecosystem; trophic levels with rising energy.
- Lösung states an interpretation in the field Beobachtung; worksheet info box gives the result away.
- Invented data without the label; axis without unit; dependent variable on x.
- Points: sub-points add to more than the task's points; AFB table not updated after a change.
- English line: "cell core" for Zellkern, "heredity information" for Erbinformation (genetic information), "sister cell" for Tochterzelle (daughter cell), "kernel", "proof" for Nachweis (test/detection), decimal comma kept.
- Animation: chromatid number changes between steps without a division; caption names a phase the picture does not show.
