# Geschichte: Fachbegriffe DE | EN

German school term with article; English = established term in English-language history teaching. German institutions without an equivalent stay German in the English line, in italics, with a gloss on first use. Glossaries in material take 5-10 terms from here and add the meaning in one line.

## Loaded terms: wording
| Term in sources / older books | In our text |
|---|---|
| Machtergreifung | „Machtergreifung“ in quotation marks, or Machtübertragung / Machtübernahme; EN: so-called "seizure of power" / transfer of power |
| Drittes Reich | NS-Staat, NS-Diktatur; „Drittes Reich“ only quoted |
| Reichskristallnacht | Novemberpogrome 1938; EN: November pogroms |
| Euthanasie | „Euthanasie“ in quotation marks, or NS-Krankenmorde; EN: so-called "euthanasia" murders |
| Endlösung, Sonderbehandlung, Volksgemeinschaft, Lebensraum | only in quotation marks with explanation of what it meant in practice |
| Zigeuner | Sinti und Roma (the old word only inside a quoted source, explained) |
| Entdeckung Amerikas | Ankunft der Europäer in Amerika / Eroberung Amerikas (KCGO: Eroberung Amerikas) |
| Eingeborene, Stämme (general) | Bevölkerung, indigene Bevölkerung, name of the people |
| Neger, Mohr and similar | never in our text; inside a source only when the task needs the passage, with a note on the term |
| Hitler's rise, "Hitler built the Autobahn" style | name actors and structures: NSDAP, elites, voters; no personalisation of structures |
| Wiedergutmachung, Vergangenheitsbewältigung | in quotation marks as contested terms (as in the KCGO) |

## False friends and traps
| German | Not | But |
|---|---|---|
| der Bürger | burgher (only medieval town) | citizen; `Bürgertum` = bourgeoisie / middle classes |
| das Reich | empire (always) | Reich; `Kaiserreich` = German Empire; Weimar: German Reich |
| der Stand | stand | estate (social order) |
| der Kanzler | chancellor of a university | Chancellor (head of government) |
| das Gymnasium, der Rat, der Landtag | gym, advice | keep German with gloss: council; state parliament |
| die Novemberrevolution | "November revolution" for Russia | German Revolution of November 1918; Russia: October Revolution |
| national | national (neutral) | context: `national gesinnt` = nationalist |
| die Quelle | spring, well | source |
| aktuell · eventuell | actual · eventually | current · possibly |
| "eine Billion" | billion | trillion |
