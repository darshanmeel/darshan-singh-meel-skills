## Not verified (carried as `check:` in the references)
- Which Themenfelder are binding in a given exam year and their Schwerpunkte: Abiturerlass of the year (kultus.hessen.de → Landesabitur).
- Abitur task formats, source lengths; the length guide in `oberstufe.md` is a working default.
- Whether and how many Klassenarbeiten Geschichte writes per year in Sek I; in which years the subject is taught (Stundentafel, school plan).
- Topics per year in Sek I: the KC gives none; the table in `sek1.md` is a working default.
- Copyright rules named in `quellenarbeit.md`: UrhG §§ 5, 51, 60a, 64 (gesetze-im-internet.de) - not read in this run; the skill's rule (public domain, official or own texts only) is stricter than the law.
- StGB §§ 86, 86a (symbols of unconstitutional organisations) - not read; the skill avoids such symbols in figures.
- Hessen rules for bilingual subject teaching.
- English terms in `begriffe.md`: established usage, own selection, no official list.
- The anchor dates in the reviewer's `checks.md` are standard dates, not re-read on official pages in this run: verify the ones a material uses.

## How to re-check
- Open only official or public scholarly pages: kultus.hessen.de, bundestag.de, bundesarchiv.de, dhm.de/lemo, bpb.de, hdg.de, gesetze-im-internet.de, state archives, memorial sites. Send no project, school or student details in a search.
- When a `check:` is resolved, change the line in the reference and move the entry into the table with document date and section.
- Not sources: textbooks, private school sites, encyclopedias edited by anyone, forums, AI answers.
