# Klassenstufen 7/8

Paper: 30 problems, 75 min, start 30, maximum 150 (`wettbewerb.md`). Problem families and limits below are this skill's working rules; the organiser publishes no syllabus.

## Curriculum (KC Hessen, Sekundarstufe I - Gymnasium, Mathematik - read, `sources.md`)
- Kompetenzbereich **Problemlösen**, lernzeitbezogene Kompetenzerwartungen am Ende der Jahrgangsstufe 8 - "Die Lernenden":
  - "erfassen in Problemsituationen mögliche mathematische Fragestellungen, formulieren diese in eigenen Worten und entwickeln Lösungsideen,"
  - "wenden heuristische Problemlösestrategien und mathematische Verfahren bewusst zur Lösung einfacher Alltagsprobleme an,"
  - "nutzen unterschiedliche Darstellungsformen und Verfahrensweisen zur Problemlösung,"
  - "entnehmen einer anwendungsbezogenen Problemstellung die zu ihrer Lösung relevanten Daten,"
  - "interpretieren Ergebnisse mit Blick auf das zu lösende Problem,"
  - "reflektieren Lösungswege."

- Cite: `KC Hessen, Sek I Gymnasium, Mathematik · Problemlösen · <Kompetenzerwartung> (Ende Jg. 8) · Inhaltsfeld <name> (Leitidee <name>)`.

## Problem families
| Points | Families |
|---|---|
| 3 | value of a term with a pattern · share or percentage of a simple figure · angle in a triangle or at parallel lines · which statement about integers is true · unit conversion with a trap · reading two diagrams together |
| 4 | linear relation from a story (two unknowns by reasoning) · area by cutting and rearranging · divisibility and remainders · counting arrangements with a restriction · average changes when a value is added · speed, time, distance by proportional reasoning · digits of a product |
| 5 | parity and invariants (operations on numbers on a board) · extremal arrangements · logic grid with 4-5 conditions · angle chase with an auxiliary line · counting paths or colourings · last digits and cycles of powers · number of solutions of a digit puzzle |

## Language
- Subject language of the level without explanation (Term, Variable, Primzahl, Mittelwert, Winkelsumme); stems up to 5 sentences.
- Variables in stems are named (`n`, `x`) and their domain stated (`natürliche Zahl`, `ganze Zahl`).

## Typical mistakes to build distractors from
- percentage of the wrong base; percentage points taken as percent
- sign errors with negative numbers; minus before a bracket
- average of averages
- proportional used where antiproportional holds
- angle read from the sketch instead of derived
- ordered counted as unordered (and the reverse)
