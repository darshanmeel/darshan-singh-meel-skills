# Biologie: experiments, safety, conventions, typical errors, animations

Safety facts: RiSU (KMK, Stand 21.09.2023), sections named in brackets; status in `sources.md`. Subject conventions and expected observations are standard school biology (not from an official page) - checked by solving, not by citation.

## Safety: who decides what
- Hessen: the Aufsichtsverordnung makes RiSU Teil I binding (kultus.hessen.de, Arbeitsschutz). check: Verordnung über die Aufsicht über Schülerinnen und Schüler, the paragraphs on science lessons.
- The Gefährdungsbeurteilung (GBU) is made and documented by the school; the teacher is responsible for it in the own lessons and must be fachkundig (RiSU I-0.1, I-0.2). Form is free; existing documents (e.g. a Versuchsbeschreibung, DEGINTU) may be used if they fit the school's conditions.
- This skill writes a **draft safety block**. Print under it: `Entwurf - ersetzt nicht die Gefährdungsbeurteilung der Lehrkraft.` / `Draft - does not replace the teacher's risk assessment.`
- Order of measures: S-T-O-P = Substitution, technische, organisatorische, personenbezogene Maßnahmen (RiSU I-0.3). Say in the plan what was substituted or why not.
- Unterweisung: general instruction at the start of each half-year, noted in Klassenbuch/Kursheft; before each experiment specific instructions on substances, handling, disposal - a Versuchsblatt counts (RiSU I-3.16).

## Rules that shape a biology experiment (RiSU)
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Students never work alone; the teacher stays in the room as a rule | I-2 (Allgemeine Verhaltensregeln) |
| No eating, drinking, make-up in the Fachraum; hands washed after work with Biostoffe | I-6.4.2 |
| Mouth pipetting forbidden; taste tests and skin application forbidden for hazardous or unknown substances | I-2, I-6.4.2 |
| Experiments on students only if harm is excluded and hygiene is ensured; **taking blood from students is not allowed**; EKG/EEG only with devices under the medical-device rules or fully off the mains | I-2 "Versuche an Schülerinnen und Schülern" |
| Tierversuche are forbidden in schools; experiments with living animals only if they cause no pain or suffering; no sick animals | I-7.1 |
| Dead vertebrates and organs (pig's eye, fish, heart, lung) only from the food trade or a slaughterhouse | I-7.1 |
| Collecting wild animals: allowed for common species with a reasonable purpose; specially protected species (§ 44 BNatSchG) need clarification | I-7.1 |
| Biostoffe: only risk group 1 for normal student work (e.g. Bäckerhefe, *E. coli* K12, Heuaufguss); risk group 2 needs Schutzstufe 2 and a substitution check | I-6.1.2, I-6.4.3 |
| Unknown mixed cultures (Abklatsch, soil, air plates): seal the Petri dish with tape after incubation, never open again; do not seal airtight during incubation (anaerobes of risk group 2 may grow) | I-6.4.2 |
| Moulds: closed handling only (sensitising) | I-6.4.2 |
| Disposal of risk group 1 cultures: sink (liquids) or household waste in a sealed bag, if the GBU allows | I-6.4.2 |
| Sharp instruments (Skalpell, Kanüle) only if needed; collect in puncture-proof containers | I-6.4.2 |
| Chemicals in biology experiments (Lugol, ethanol, hydrogen peroxide, limewater, solvents): classification and student restrictions as in chemistry - look up DEGINTU; only extremely flammable liquids (H224) are not allowed for students in Jg. 5-9; up to Jg. 4 no flammable liquids (RiSU I-3.6.2, Tabelle 3b) | I-3.6; check: DEGINTU entry per substance |

- Human samples (cheek cells, saliva for amylase): own sample only, voluntary, disposable tools, disposal as the GBU says - check: RiSU I-2 ("Versuche an Schülerinnen und Schülern") and the school's GBU before planning; a bought amylase solution or onion cells avoid the question (substitution).
- Pregnancy: restrictions apply to teachers and students (RiSU I-3.7, I-6); never ask or name a student in material - the plan says "Mutterschutz in der GBU geprüft".

## Safety block on every experiment sheet
| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| Versuchsart | Schülerversuch (EA/PA/GA) or Lehrerdemonstration |
| Stoffe und Biostoffe | name, concentration, amount; GHS pictogram code + signal word from DEGINTU/Sicherheitsdatenblatt, or "kein Gefahrstoff"; Biostoff with risk group |
| Gefahren | what can happen in this experiment (cut, burn, splash, allergy, infection) |
| Schutz | Schutzbrille, Handschuhe, Haare zusammenbinden, tools, hygiene |
| Entsorgung | where each residue goes |
| Hinweis | the draft sentence above |

Unverified classifications are written as `laut Sicherheitsdatenblatt prüfen / check against the safety data sheet`, never as fact.

## Standard experiments and expected observations
| Topic (level) | Experiment | Expected observation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zelle (Jg. 7-10, E.1) | Mikroskopieren: Zwiebelhäutchen, Wasserpestblatt | Zellwand, Zellkern (onion; with staining), Chloroplasten and Plasmaströmung (Elodea) | drawing rules below |
| Osmose (E.1) | Plasmolyse: rote Zwiebel in konzentrierter Salz- oder Zuckerlösung | Protoplast löst sich von der Zellwand, Vakuole schrumpft, Farbe wird dunkler; mit Wasser reversibel (Deplasmolyse) | KCGO: "experimentell" |
| Osmose (E.1) | Kartoffelzylinder in Wasser und in Salzlösungen, Masse vorher/nachher | Masse nimmt in Wasser zu, in konzentrierter Lösung ab; dazwischen eine Konzentration ohne Änderung | quantitative: diagram mass change vs concentration |
| Enzyme (E.2) | Katalase: Kartoffel oder Hefe + Wasserstoffperoxid-Lösung (verdünnt) | Schäumen, Gasblasen; Glimmspan flammt auf (Sauerstoff); gekochte Probe: keine Reaktion | H₂O₂: check DEGINTU for the concentration used |
| Enzyme (E.2) | Amylase + Stärkelösung, Iod-Kaliumiodid-Probe in Zeitabständen | Blaufärbung wird schwächer und bleibt aus; im Eisbad langsamer, gekochtes Enzym: Blaufärbung bleibt | one variable per series (Temperatur or pH) |
| Fotosynthese (Jg. 7-10, Q3.2) | Sauerstoffbildung bei Wasserpest: Blasen pro Minute bei verschiedenem Lampenabstand | mehr Blasen bei kleinerem Abstand, bis zur Sättigung | heat filter or LED; count 3 times, mean |
| Fotosynthese (Jg. 7-10) | Stärkenachweis im teilweise abgedeckten Blatt | belichtete Teile blauschwarz, abgedeckte nicht | heißes Ethanol: Lehrerdemonstration, Wasserbad, keine offene Flamme |
| Fotosynthese (Q3.2) | Chromatografie von Blattfarbstoffen | Banden: Carotine (gelb-orange, vorn), Xanthophylle (gelb), Chlorophyll a (blaugrün), Chlorophyll b (gelbgrün) | solvent mix: check DEGINTU; Abzug |
| Gärung (Q3.2 LK) | Hefe + Zuckerlösung, Gärröhrchen oder Luftballon | Gasbildung (CO₂), stärker bei ca. 30-35 °C; ohne Zucker kaum | risk group 1 |
| Atmung (Jg. 5-8) | Puls und Atemfrequenz vor und nach Belastung | beide steigen, fallen in der Erholung | voluntary; no competition; health conditions respected without naming anyone |
| Sinne, Nerven (Jg. 7-10) | Reaktionszeit mit fallendem Lineal; blinder Fleck | Fallstrecke wird mit Übung kleiner; Punkt verschwindet bei bestimmtem Abstand | harmless |
| Genetik (Jg. 9-10, Q1) | DNA-Extraktion aus Obst (Spülmittel, Salz, kaltes Ethanol) | weiße, fädige Schlieren an der Grenzschicht | ethanol: flammable (H225), no open flame; allowed for students from Jg. 5 (only H224 liquids are barred in Jg. 5-9); check: DEGINTU |
| Mikrobiologie (Jg. 7-10) | Abklatsch- oder Luftkeimplatten | Kolonien verschiedener Form und Farbe nach 2-3 Tagen | sealed dishes only (rule above) |
| Ökologie (Q3.1) | Erfassung abiotischer Faktoren und Arten in einem Areal | Daten je Standort | Aufsicht outdoors, protected species |

A sheet states the expected observation only in the Lösung and in the lesson plan, never on the student sheet.

## Protocol form (Versuchsprotokoll)
`Fragestellung` → `Hypothese` (with reason) → `Material` → `Sicherheit` → `Durchführung` (numbered, with control and the one variable changed) → `Beobachtung` (what is seen, measured; table) → `Auswertung / Deutung` (explanation, back to the hypothesis) → `Fehlerbetrachtung`.
- Jg. 5-6: Frage, Vermutung, Versuch und Kontrollversuch, Beobachtung, Ergebnis.
- English lines: question · hypothesis · materials · safety · procedure · observation · evaluation / interpretation · sources of error.

## Conventions
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Microscopic drawing | pencil, at least half a page, clear closed lines, no shading; labels at the right with ruled lines; title, object, magnification (Okular × Objektiv), date |
| Diagram | title; x = independent variable; axes with quantity and unit; measured points marked; curve only where a trend is meant |
| Chromosomes | human body cell 2n = 46, gamete n = 23. Count chromosomes by centromeres; say "Zwei-Chromatid-Chromosom" / "Ein-Chromatid-Chromosom" |
| Crossing scheme | P, F₁, F₂; dominant allele capital, recessive small (A, a); genotype and phenotype named separately; gonosomal: X^A X^a, X^a Y |
| Pedigree | circle = female, square = male, filled = affected; generations I, II, III; persons numbered; invented families only |
| Nucleic acids | strands with 5' and 3' ends; mRNA codons read 5'→3'; Code-Sonne from the inside outwards |
| Equations | Fotosynthese: 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ (Lichtenergie, Chlorophyll); Zellatmung: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O; atoms balanced, energy named |
| Values that differ by textbook | ATP yield of cell respiration, Ruhepotenzial (about −70 mV), duration of an Aktionspotenzial: write "ca." and use the value of the class's book - ask or state the assumption |
| Names | species in italics, genus capital (*Elodea canadensis*); German common name first in Sek I |
| Language | "angepasst sein / Angepasstheit" (state), not "sich anpassen" for evolution; "Individuen mit ... haben mehr Nachkommen" instead of "die Art will ..." |

## Typical student errors (use in "Typischer Fehler" and in distractors)
| Field | Error | Right |
|---|---|---|
| Zelle | "Pflanzenzellen haben keine Mitochondrien" | both have mitochondria; plants add chloroplasts, cell wall, vacuole |
| Osmose | "Salz zieht in die Zelle" | water moves through the membrane towards the higher solute concentration |
| Enzyme | "Enzyme werden verbraucht" / "bei Kälte zerstört" | catalysts are reused; cold slows (reversible), heat denatures |
| Fotosynthese, Atmung | "Pflanzen atmen nur nachts" / "Pflanzen atmen CO₂" | cell respiration runs day and night; photosynthesis only in light |
| Mitose, Meiose | chromatids counted as chromosomes; "Mitose halbiert den Chromosomensatz" | mitosis keeps 2n; meiosis halves to n |
| Genetik | "dominant = häufig"; gene, allele and trait mixed up | dominance says which allele shows in the heterozygote, not how common it is |
| Evolution | "Tiere passen sich an, weil sie es brauchen"; "der Mensch stammt vom Affen ab" | variation exists first, selection acts on it; humans and today's apes share ancestors |
| Ökologie | "Energie kreist im Ökosystem" | matter cycles, energy flows and is degraded to heat |
| Neurobiologie | "ein starkes Signal = größeres Aktionspotenzial" | all-or-none; strength is coded by frequency |
| Immunbiologie | antibody, antigen, antibiotic mixed up | antigen = foreign structure; antibody = protein that binds it; antibiotic = drug against bacteria |
