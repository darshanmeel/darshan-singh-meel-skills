## How to cite
`KC Hessen, Sek I Gymnasium, Mathematik` · Kompetenzbereich · Kompetenzerwartung with stage `(Ende Jg. 6)`, `(Ende Jg. 8)` or `(Ende Jg. 9/10)` · Leitidee → Inhaltsfeld → Schwerpunkt of the double year.
- The KC fixes content per double year (5/6, 7/8, 9/10), not per year, and allows shifts across the borders "sofern die Anforderungen am Ende der Jahrgangsstufe 9/10 erreicht werden". The year of a topic comes from the school's Fachcurriculum: ask, or place by the table below and state the placement in the Stundenentwurf as a fact (`Einordnung in Jg. 6; das KC legt Doppeljahrgänge fest`).
- Kompetenzerwartungen are cumulative: what is expected at the end of Jg. 6 is assumed later and not repeated.

## Rules for Sek I material
- **Level of numbers**: Jg. 5/6 natural numbers, fractions and decimals without calculator - choose numbers that work by hand (denominators with small kgV, divisions that end). From the introduction of the calculator on, realistic data may be "ugly"; then state the rounding (`Runde auf zwei Nachkommastellen`).
- **Grundvorstellungen first**: a rule appears after a picture or action that carries it (fraction as part of a whole before the fraction rule; balance before equation rules; rectangle area before binomial formula).
- **Notation** as in `konventionen.md`: `·` and `:` for multiplication and division, decimal comma, `P(2|3)`, mixed numbers only for results.
- **Solutions** show every transformation step with the operation at the right (`| − 3x`), a Probe for equations, an Antwortsatz for Sachaufgaben, the unit in every line of a Größen calculation.
- **Proofs and reasons** (Argumentieren): from Jg. 7 on at least one task per worksheet asks `Begründe` with an argument that is more than a calculation.
- **Klassenarbeiten**: number per year and procedure - base `bewertung.md`; with a hilfsmittelfreier Teil when the school does so (ask).

## Typical mistakes (for "Typischer Fehler" and as test distractors)
| Topic | Mistake |
|---|---|
| Brüche addieren | numerators and denominators added separately: ½ + ⅓ = 2/5 |
| Brüche vergleichen | larger denominator read as larger fraction |
| Dezimalbrüche | 0,25 > 0,3 "because 25 > 3"; 0,5 · 0,5 = 2,5 |
| Negative Zahlen | −3 − 5 = −2; minus times minus = minus |
| Prozentrechnung | Grundwert confused after a change: +20 % then −20 % = "same again" |
| Terme | 3 + 4x = 7x; (a + b)² = a² + b²; minus before the bracket not distributed |
| Gleichungen | operation on one side only; division by the variable loses a solution |
| Proportionalität | "more → more" taken as proportional; Dreisatz for antiproportional data |
| Lineare Funktionen | slope and intercept swapped; slope read as Δx/Δy |
| Quadratische Gleichungen | x² = 9 → x = 3 only; p-q formula with wrong sign of p |
| Pythagoras | applied in a non-right triangle; hypotenuse not identified |
| Flächen / Volumen | unit factors: 1 m² = 100 cm²; scaling a length by 2 "doubles" the area |
| Wahrscheinlichkeit | path probabilities added along a path; "ohne Zurücklegen" ignored |
| Statistik | mean and median mixed; mean of means without weights |
