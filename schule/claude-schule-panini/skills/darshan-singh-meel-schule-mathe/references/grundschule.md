## How to cite
`KC Hessen, Primarstufe, Mathematik` · Kompetenzbereich · Bildungsstandard `(Ende Jg. 4)` · Inhaltsfeld + Schwerpunkt.
- The KC sets standards for the end of Jg. 4 only; for Jg. 1/2 it describes the development (6.2) and names two anchors: Zahlenraum bis 100 and Größen Geldwerte, Längen, Zeitspannen. ## Rules for Grundschule material
- **Numbers inside the Zahlenraum of the year**, results too (also intermediate results). No negative results, no remainders before division with Rest is taught, no decimals before Größen in comma notation (`3,50 €`) are introduced.
- **E-I-S**: every new idea in the order handelnd (material: Plättchen, Zehnerstangen, Rechenrahmen) → bildlich (picture of the same material) → symbolisch (number sentence). A worksheet shows the picture step before the bare calculation; the lesson plan names the material.
- **Representations** the class knows: Zwanzigerfeld, Hunderterfeld, Hundertertafel, Zahlenstrahl, Stellenwerttafel (T H Z E), Rechendreieck, Zahlenmauer. Draw them as SVG to scale; do not describe them in words.
- **Language**: sentences up to about 10 words, one action per sentence, present tense, no nominal chains; name persons by invented first names; `Rechne`, `Zeichne`, `Erkläre`, `Begründe` (from Jg. 3) - base `operatoren.md`.
- **Sachaufgaben**: Frage - Rechnung - Antwort; realistic prices and sizes for children; all data needed, at most one surplus datum from Jg. 3 on (and say so in the solution).
- **Answer space**: squared field (`.karo`) for every calculation; written algorithms need one square per digit; lines (`.zeile`, 12 mm) for sentences. A page at 14 pt holds one info box plus one task, or two to three tasks - plan the page breaks.
- **English line**: one short line per German line; none inside number work, place value tables or pictures.
- **Tests**: length and number per year - base `bewertung.md` (Jg. 2: up to 15 min; Jg. 3: up to 30 min; Jg. 4: up to one lesson). Mostly AFB I and II, one AFB III task.
- **Differentiation**: same task type on three levels - smaller numbers or material picture (★), standard (★★), open or reversed task (★★★: Zahlenmauer with gaps, "Finde alle ...", "Warum ...?").

## Typical mistakes (use them in "Typischer Fehler" and as distractors)
| Topic | Mistake | Shows as |
|---|---|---|
| Schriftliche Addition | Übertrag vergessen or at the wrong place | 438 + 247 = 675 |
| Schriftliche Subtraktion | smaller digit subtracted from the larger in every column | 523 − 178 = 455 |
| Stellenwert | digits swapped when speaking/writing (Zahlendreher), zero left out | "dreihundertfünf" → 35 |
| Einmaleins | neighbour result (7 · 8 = 54), addition instead of multiplication | 6 · 0 = 6 |
| Division | remainder larger than the divisor; zero in the quotient left out | 612 : 6 = 12 |
| Größen | unit conversion with the wrong factor; comma read as separator of two numbers | 1 m 5 cm = 1,5 m |
| Uhrzeit | Zeitpunkt and Zeitspanne mixed; calculating in tens across the hour | 7:50 + 20 min = 7:70 |
| Umfang / Fläche | perimeter and area mixed; squares counted along the edge | Umfang = Kästchen innen |
| Sachaufgaben | all numbers of the text combined with the last operation practised | surplus datum used |
