## Not verified (carried as `check:` or `prüfen`)
- GHS classification, signal word, H and P statements of any substance or solution: DEGINTU (`https://degintu.dguv.de`) or the supplier's safety data sheet, per substance and concentration. Nothing in this skill states a classification as fact.
- The meaning and layout of the nine GHS pictograms follow the CLP regulation (EC) No 1272/2008; the regulation text was not opened: check: `https://echa.europa.eu` (CLP pictograms) if a detail matters.
- Which content inside Q2.1, Q2.2, Q3.1-Q3.3 and Q4.1 is binding for an exam year, exam format and permitted aids: Abiturerlass of that year (kultus.hessen.de → Landesabitur).
- In which Jahrgang Chemie starts at the user's school (Stundentafel, school decision).
- The school's Entsorgungskonzept (which container for which residue).
- Bilingual Chemie (Bili) in Hessen: no rule text was read; English lines follow the base rule, the glossary is our own.
- Standard potentials, pK<sub>S</sub> values, atomic masses: usual school table values, not taken from an official page; tasks print the values they use.

## Not from official pages
- Notation rules (IUPAC conventions as taught in school), calculation form, expected observations, typical errors and the term list are standard school chemistry written for this skill; correctness is checked by solving each task (SKILL.md step 5), not by citation.
