## Not verified (carried as `check:` in the references)
- Which Themenfelder beyond 1 and 2 (Q1-Q3) and which Q4 field are binding in a given exam year; Schwerpunkte: Abiturerlass of the year (kultus.hessen.de → Landesabitur).
- Abitur task formats and permitted aids in Geographie (Atlas).
- Whether and how many Klassenarbeiten Erdkunde writes per year in Sek I (VOGSV, school rule).
- Topics per year in Sek I: the KC gives none; the table in `sek1.md` is a working default.
- Terms of use of data providers named in `fachpraxis.md` (Destatis, Eurostat, UN, World Bank, WMO, USGS, GFZ, IPCC, UBA, BKG, Natural Earth) and of the DWD open data (source line required; read the DWD terms before publishing).
- Hessen rules for bilingual subject teaching.
- English terms in `begriffe.md`: established UK school usage, own selection, no official list.
- Walter/Lieth conventions (10 °C = 20 mm, compression above 100 mm, N < 2T): standard school convention, not from an official Hessen text.

## How to re-check
- Open only the official pages above. Send no project, school or student details in a search.
- When a `check:` is resolved, change the line in the reference and move the entry into the table with document date and section.
- Not sources: textbooks, private school sites, travel and weather portals, encyclopedias edited by anyone.
