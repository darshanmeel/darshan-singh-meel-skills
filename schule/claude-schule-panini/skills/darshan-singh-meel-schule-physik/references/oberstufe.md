Source: Kerncurriculum gymnasiale Oberstufe Physik, Ausgabe 2024, Stand 01.08.2025 (PDF, read; `sources.md`). Wording of Themenfelder and standards as printed. Formulas are printed as images in the PDF and were not machine-readable: the formulas in the last column are the usual forms - check: KCGO PDF for the exact symbols before quoting a formula as "KCGO".

## Rules of the KCGO
- E-Phase: Themenfelder E.1-E.3 binding; at least one binding Themenfeld in the second half-year; about two thirds of the teaching time for the binding fields.
- Q-Phase: Q1: Themenfelder 1-2 · Q2: 1-3 · Q3: 1-3 · Q4: 1 → nine binding Themenfelder for the written Abitur. An Erlass can set priorities inside them; parts of a Themenfeld may move between neighbouring half-years.
- "zum Beispiel" = suggestion; "am Beispiel" = binding.
- grundlegendes Niveau = GK and LK; erhöhtes Niveau = LK only. Bildungsstandards are the same for GK and LK.
- Learning in contexts (research, technology, society, everyday life) is expected: every unit names its context.
- Basiskonzepte (from the KMK Bildungsstandards Physik 2020): Erhaltung und Gleichgewicht · Superposition und Komponenten · Mathematisieren und Vorhersagen · Zufall und Determiniertheit.

## Landesabitur (frame; changes per exam year)
- Abiturerlass Landesabitur 2027, Physik: materialgebundene Aufgabe after the KMK standards; three independent Aufgabenvorschläge are worked on; at most 90 BE (GK), 120 BE (LK). Exam focus: Q1.1, Q1.2, Q2.1, Q2.2, Q2.3, Q3.1, Q3.2, Q3.3, Q4.1.
- Operators: the Landesabitur list for Biologie, Chemie, Informatik, Mathematik, Physik (base `operatoren.md`), Sie-form.
- Aids, formula collection, calculator type: check: "Hinweise für die Fächer Mathematik, Chemie, Physik und Informatik" of the exam year (kultus.hessen.de → Landesabitur → Materialien). Not read.
- Klausur length, number, percent → points: base `bewertung.md`. Klausuren in Q-Phase use material (data table, diagram, set-up sketch) and all three AFB.
