# Subject checks P1-P10 (in addition to the base checklist 1-12)

Each check: what to look at · Must when. Evidence for every finding: file, task id or line, quoted text.

| # | Check | Look at | Must when |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Curriculum | Sek I: Kompetenzbereich is one of Analyse-, Urteils-, Handlungs-, Methodenkompetenz; Kompetenzerwartung quoted as printed with `(Ende Jg. 9/10)`; Inhaltsfeld is one of the six. Oberstufe: standard code exists (A1-A12, U1-U12, H1-H9, M1-M6); Themenfeld number and title match; e-level standards and erhöhtes-Niveau content only in LK; subject (PoWi vs. Wirtschaftswissenschaften) matches the Themenfeld numbering | invented or misquoted KC wording; wrong Themenfeld number; LK-only content in GK material |
| P2 | Neutrality (Beutelsbacher Konsens) | run the 6-point neutrality test of `fachregeln.md`: wording, balance of positions, parties and persons, solution, schools of thought, stereotypes | material or solution pushes a position; a controversial question shown as settled; selection of parties without a rule |
| P3 | Real data | every real figure: source line with institution, table/publication, Stand, Abrufdatum; unit, area, period; percent vs. percentage points; rounding as in the source; newest release | no source or date; value differs from the source; unit or period missing so the task is ambiguous |
| P4 | Model data | label `Modellzahlen (erfunden)` with English line; internally consistent (rows fit the curve, flows balance); not mixed with real data; invented persons, firms, towns marked | model data presented as real; inconsistent rows that change a solution |
| P5 | Institutions and law | every statement about organs, procedures, competences, articles, laws: correct and with version date where quoted; office holders and majorities dated or absent; bodies not confused (Europäischer Rat / Rat der EU / Europarat; Bundestag / Bundesregierung) | wrong rule, wrong article, outdated fact shown as current |
| P6 | Models and diagrams | axes (Preis on y, Menge on x), labels, units; curves by line style and label; equilibrium at the intersection; shift vs. movement; cycle flows opposite and balanced; assumptions and at least one limit stated | diagram or model mechanism wrong; numbers in figure, table, solution and animation differ |
| P7 | Material | every AFB II/III task refers to numbered material; text length fits the level; material carries what the task needs; origin stated (`für diese Aufgabe verfasst`, public source with date); no copied cartoon or press text | task cannot be solved from material + taught content; copied protected material |
| P8 | Judgement tasks | criteria named or demanded; Sachurteil before Werturteil; Erwartungshorizont lists criteria, arguments for both directions, and awards points for reasoning; operator fits (beurteilen / bewerten / erörtern) | points depend on the position taken; no criteria anywhere; operator and expected performance do not match |
| P9 | Operators and AFB | operators from the FB II list (Oberstufe: Sie-form; Sek I: du-form, level list of `sek1.md`); Klausur rises I → II → III; AFB III present in an Oberstufe Klausur | as base area 3 |
| P10 | Subject terms DE/EN | terms as in `begriffe.md`; German institutions keep their name with the English term in brackets; no false friends (Konjunktur, aktuell, Bundesland); `N / D`, `A / S` labels consistent | wrong term changes the meaning (e.g. demand vs. quantity demanded, Council of Europe vs. European Council) |

## Recompute list
- Market tables: each row against the stated curve; equilibrium by `N(p) = A(p)`; excess demand / supply at each named price.
- Circular flow with numbers: inflows = outflows per sector.
- Multiplier: `1 / (1 − c)`; rounds add up.
- Growth and inflation: `(new − old) / old`; percentage points for rate differences; real = nominal − inflation only as an approximation.
- Budget shares: parts add up to 100 % (or the remainder is named).
- Seat or vote shares: sums; the method named.
- Test: parts add up to the total; AFB shares; grade key thresholds = percentage × total, in whole or half points.

## Wording of typical findings
- `Neutralität` - quote the loaded word or the missing counter-position; fix: neutral wording or the added position with source.
- `Daten` - quote the figure; fix: source line, or replace by model data with label.
- `Fachfehler` - quote the statement; give the correct rule and where to verify it.
- `Modell` - name the wrong element (axis, direction of flow, shift); give the corrected picture in words.
- Bilingual findings: kinds and format as in base `references/review.md`.
