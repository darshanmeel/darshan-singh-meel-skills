# Klassenstufen 5/6

Paper: 24 problems, 75 min, start 24, maximum 120 (`wettbewerb.md`). Problem families and limits below are this skill's working rules; the organiser publishes no syllabus.

## Curriculum (KC Hessen, Sekundarstufe I - Gymnasium, Mathematik - read, `sources.md`)
- Kompetenzbereich **Problemlösen**, lernzeitbezogene Kompetenzerwartungen am Ende der Jahrgangsstufe 6 - "Die Lernenden":
  - "erfassen in Problemsituationen mögliche mathematische Fragestellungen, formulieren diese in eigenen Worten und entwickeln Lösungsideen,"
  - "wenden heuristische Problemlösestrategien und mathematische Verfahren zur Lösung einfacher Alltagsprobleme an,"
  - "entnehmen einer anwendungsbezogenen Problemstellung die zu ihrer Lösung relevanten Daten,"
  - "interpretieren Ergebnisse mit Blick auf das zu lösende Problem,"
  - "reflektieren Lösungswege."
- Sek I structure: five **Leitideen**, each split into **Inhaltsfelder**:

  | Leitidee | Inhaltsfelder |
  |---|---|
  | Zahl und Operation | Zahlen · Operationen und ihre Eigenschaften |
  | Raum und Form | Ebene Figuren · Körper · Beziehungen zwischen geometrischen Objekten |
  | Größen und Messen | Umgang mit Größen · Messvorgänge |
  | Funktionaler Zusammenhang | Zuordnungen und ihre Darstellungen · Funktionen und Gleichungen |
  | Daten und Zufall | statistische Erhebungen und ihre Auswertung · Umgang mit dem Zufall |

- Cite: `KC Hessen, Sek I Gymnasium, Mathematik · Problemlösen · <Kompetenzerwartung> (Ende Jg. 6) · Inhaltsfeld <name> (Leitidee <name>)`.

## Knowledge that may be used (KC 7.3, column Jahrgangsstufe 5/6)
| Leitidee | Allowed | Keep out |
|---|---|---|
| Zahl und Operation | natural numbers; divisibility, divisors and multiples, ggT, kgV, primes; fractions (part of a whole, of several wholes, as measure, as ratio); decimals; simple percentages; rounding; rules for clever calculation | calculating with negative numbers, algebra with terms |
| Raum und Form | square, rectangle, triangle, circle as shapes; composite areas; line and rotational symmetry; coordinate system, first quadrant; cuboid, cube, composite solids, nets, oblique views; parallel, perpendicular, distance | area of triangle and circle by formula, Pythagoras |
| Größen und Messen | length, mass, money, time span, angle; perimeter and area of square and rectangle; volume and surface of cuboid and cube; unit conversion; estimating | area formulas of other figures |
| Funktionaler Zusammenhang | assignments of quantities in tables, diagrams and words | Dreisatz as a method, equations solved formally |
| Daten und Zufall | absolute and relative frequencies, arithmetic mean, median, range, charts; chance in everyday situations | tree diagrams, path rules |

- Klasse 5 sits the same paper and has, by March, mostly natural numbers, quantities and basic geometry. 3-point problems need no fractions, decimals or percentages; in 4- and 5-point problems fractions appear only as simple parts (half, third, quarter). check: the school's Fachcurriculum for the order of topics in Jg. 5 and 6.
- An unknown is found by working backwards or trying, not by formal equation solving.

## Problem families
| Points | Families |
|---|---|
| 3 | clever calculation with a pattern · perimeter of a figure made of squares · weekday or clock after n steps · reading a diagram · which number has a property (divisible, digit sum) · mirror image or rotation of a figure |
| 4 | working backwards over 3 steps · counting numbers with a digit property · rectangle cut into squares or equal rectangles · ages and differences · cube nets: which faces are opposite · remainders and periodic patterns · sharing given by a story |
| 5 | truth and lie statements (4 persons) · extreme case with digit cards · counting squares or rectangles in a grid under a condition · sums in magic figures · painted cube cut into small cubes · tournaments and handshakes · last digit of a product |

## Language
- Sentences up to about 15 words; subject terms from class (Umfang, Flächeninhalt, Quersumme, Teiler, Vielfaches) used without explanation, others explained in the stem.
- At most 5 sentences per stem.

## Typical mistakes to build distractors from
- perimeter of a composite figure = sum of the single perimeters
- area and perimeter mixed up
- remainder and quotient mixed up; start day counted as day 1
- leading zeros counted as digits; "zweistellig" includes 01-09
- inverse steps in the original order
- one case of a systematic list missing (the first or the last)
