# Klassenstufen 3/4

Paper: 24 problems, 75 min, start 24, maximum 120 (`wettbewerb.md`). Template: `<body class="gs">` (14 pt) on student files, not on key files (`formate.md`). Problem families and limits below are this skill's working rules; the organiser publishes no syllabus.

## Curriculum (KC Hessen, Primarstufe, Mathematik - read, `sources.md`)
- Kompetenzbereich **Problemlösen**, Bildungsstandards am Ende der Jahrgangsstufe 4 - "Die Lernenden können":
  - "in Problemsituationen mögliche mathematische Fragestellungen und Zusammenhänge erfassen und diese in eigenen Worten formulieren,"
  - "Lösungsstrategien entwickeln und auf ähnliche Sachverhalte übertragen,"
  - "Ergebnisse reflektieren,"
  - "Lösungswege reflektieren."
- Inhaltsfelder (= Leitideen in the Primarstufe): Muster und Strukturen · Zahl und Operation · Raum und Form · Größen und Messen · Daten und Zufall.
- Cite: `KC Hessen, Primarstufe, Mathematik · Problemlösen · <standard> (Ende Jg. 4) · <Inhaltsfeld>`.

## Knowledge that may be used
| Inhaltsfeld | Allowed (KC Schwerpunktsetzungen) | Keep out |
|---|---|---|
| Zahl und Operation | natural numbers (KC: Zahlenraum bis 1.000.000 at the end of Jg. 4), place value, four basic operations, inverse operations, simple combinatorics by trying or listing | fractions and decimals as numbers, negative numbers, variables, written division with large numbers |
| Muster und Strukturen | number and shape patterns, continuing sequences, simple proportional reasoning in tables | formulas for the n-th term |
| Raum und Form | plane figures and solids, line symmetry, views and plans, cube buildings, perimeter, area by covering with unit squares, volume by unit cubes | area formulas, angles in degrees |
| Größen und Messen | money, lengths, time spans, weights; converting everyday units; everyday fractions of measures (halbe Stunde, Viertelliter) | compound units, speed formulas |
| Daten und Zufall | tables and bar charts, "sicher, möglich, unmöglich", simple chances | probabilities as fractions |

- Klasse 3 sits the same paper: 3-point problems stay within numbers up to 1000 and need no written multiplication or division. check: the school's Jahrgangsplan for what Klasse 3 has covered by March.
- Results are small whole numbers; mental arithmetic only.

## Problem families
| Points | Families |
|---|---|
| 3 | one- or two-step story with small numbers · which picture fits (mirror image, missing piece) · count objects in a picture · continue a simple pattern · read a table or clock · compare lengths or weights |
| 4 | working backwards over 2-3 steps · order from clues (taller, faster, older) · count figures systematically (triangles, squares) · cube buildings: how many cubes · calendar and weekday counting · number puzzles with digits · equal sharing with a condition |
| 5 | two conditions at once (heads and legs) by assuming and adjusting · counting digits in a range · who tells the truth (3-4 statements) · paths in a grid · balance puzzles with two scales · folding and cutting paper · smallest or largest number from digit cards |

## Language
- Sentences up to about 10 words, one idea each; present tense; no nominal chains, no subjunctive in the stem.
- At most 5 sentences per stem, the question included; numbers as digits; names with 3-5 letters.
- English line: equally short; "you" forms.
- Options: single words or numbers, never sentences longer than five words.

## Typical mistakes to build distractors from
- adds all numbers in the text; ignores "je" (each)
- stops after the first step
- counts only the small figures
- counts posts instead of gaps (and the reverse)
- mixes up "mehr als" and "so viele wie"
- answers the other quantity (chickens instead of cows)
