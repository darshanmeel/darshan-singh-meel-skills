## Not verified (carried as `check:` in the references)
- Scoring of several marks for one problem; what an unreadable entry counts as.
- Prize thresholds of the coming round (the page shows the last round's; they change each year).
- KC Sek I Gymnasium, table 7.3 (p. 26), Leitidee Zahl und Operation: real numbers (roots, π), binomials and powers with rational exponents are printed in the 7/8 column; the 9/10 cell is empty, so a merged cell cannot be excluded (table lines are not in the text layer). Pythagoras, Thales, similarity and Strahlensätze, circle area and circumference, Kreiszylinder, two-stage experiments and Pfadregeln are 7/8: their rows carry separate 9/10 entries.
- KCGO Mathematik (Oberstufe): not read for this skill; `klassen-11-13.md` points to `darshan-singh-meel-schule-mathe` or the PDF.
- Which topics a class has had by the competition date (school's Fachcurriculum / Jahrgangsplan).
- Whether the Kangaroo rules of other countries match (other national organisers set their own start points, levels and names); this skill covers the German competition.

- Late topics of Klassen 7/8 (KC 7/8 content kept out of 3- and 4-point problems because Klasse 7 sits the same paper): `klassen-07-08.md`.

- Expected value of guessing: computed from the official deduction rule.

## Not sources
- Private training sites, forums, problem collections of third parties, textbooks, AI-generated problem sets.
- Past papers are a style reference for the user and are linked, never copied.
