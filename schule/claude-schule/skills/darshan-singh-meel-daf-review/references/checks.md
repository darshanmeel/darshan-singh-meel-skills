## Solving protocol (step 3 of the process)
Keep this table while working; it goes into the report only where it shows a finding.

| Task | Own answer | Key | Same? | Time (learner estimate) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Learner time = own time × 3 for closed items at A1-A2, × 2.5 from B1; writing: words ÷ rate in `bewertung.md`.
- Open tasks: write your own short answer at level, then test the key's criteria on it: can the points be awarded without guessing?

## Language check of a German sentence (D2)
1. Find the finite verb: position 2 in the main clause, last in the Nebensatz, Satzklammer closed?
2. Each noun phrase: gender → article → case required by verb or preposition → ending of article and adjective.
3. Each participle and auxiliary; each separable verb.
4. Commas, capitals, `ß/ss`, umlauts.
5. Register: du/Sie consistent; greeting and closing fit.
6. Would a native speaker say it in this situation? Textbook-correct but unnatural German is Should.

## Print view (base areas 9, 10)
- Render every file as A4 (browser print preview, or a headless browser with print-to-PDF when one is installed). Not possible → list it under "Not checked".
- Check: figure and animation labels complete and not crossed by frames · no box or single table row alone on a last page · tables of points not split · Erklärung at most 2 pages · the six to eight animation key frames show the states the captions name, the last frame equals the worksheet sentence.
- Count words of every reading text and Hörtext with a script, not by eye, before comparing with `niveaus.md`.

## Not in scope
- Rewriting the material (only on request).
- Judging the official exams or the GER.
- Facts marked `check:` in the DaF references: report as Should "verify with <page>", never confirm them from memory.
