# Fachbegriffe DE | EN

Use in glossaries and English lines. German institutions keep their German name in the English line on first use, with the English term in brackets: `the Bundestag (federal parliament)`. English terms follow standard school economics / civics usage; official English names of German bodies: check: the body's own English pages.

| Politik (as subject content) | politic | politics (process), policy (content), polity (structure) |
| Bundesland | federal country | federal state, Land |
| Kanzler | chancellor of the exchequer | Federal Chancellor |
| Sozialversicherung | social security (US sense) | social insurance |
| Marktwirtschaft | market economics | market economy |
| aktuell | actual | current |
| eventuell | eventually | possibly |
| Mehrwertsteuer | more-value tax | value added tax (VAT) |
