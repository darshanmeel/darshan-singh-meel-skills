# Grammar progression A1-C1

Working progression in the order most courses and the Goethe/telc inventories follow. Not an official list: check: "Profile deutsch" and the Prüfungsziele/Testbeschreibung of the exam for the level (`sources.md`). Material may use everything up to its level; the "not yet" column is forbidden in texts, tasks and keys of that level (fixed chunks excepted, see below).

## Rules for using the progression
- **Chunks** may precede the system: `Ich hätte gern ...`, `Könnten Sie ...?`, `Wie geht es Ihnen?`, `im Büro`, `am Montag` at A1. Mark them in the plan as "Chunk, nicht erklären".
- **Receptive before productive**: a structure may appear in a reading text one half-level before learners must produce it; then gloss it or keep it out of the items.
- **One new structure per lesson**, shown in a text first, then noticed (learners find the forms), then the rule, then practice from closed to open.
- **Rule in a table or picture**, at most 3 lines of rule text; exceptions only when the lesson's texts contain them.
- **Terms**: German grammar terms with the English term on first use in a lesson (`der Nebensatz | subordinate clause`). Full list: `aufgaben.md`.

## Facts to verify in every German sentence written
| Point | Check |
|---|---|
| Artikel, Plural | every noun in tasks, keys and glossary (`das Attest, die Atteste`) |
| Perfekt | `haben`/`sein`; Partizip form (`angerufen`, `verschoben`, `passiert`, `geblieben`) |
| Kasus | after each preposition and verb (`bei + Dativ`, `für + Akkusativ`, `helfen + Dativ`) |
| Wortstellung | Verb position 2; Satzklammer; Nebensatz: finite verb last; trennbares Verb in Nebensatz together (`anrufe`); after a Nebensatz in position 1 the verb follows directly |
| Komma | before `weil, dass, wenn, ob, obwohl` and relative clauses; before `aber, sondern, denn` |
| Rechtschreibung | `ß` after long vowel and diphthong (`Straße, heißen`), `ss` after short vowel (`dass, müssen`); capitals for nouns and formal `Sie, Ihnen, Ihr` |
