## Not verified (carried as `check:` in the references)
- Wording of all GER descriptors: `niveaus.md` gives own summaries marked `sinngemäß`.
- Grammar and vocabulary inventories per level: working progression, not an official list (Profile deutsch; Goethe Testbeschreibungen per level were not read except A1).
- Goethe: A1 Fit in Deutsch 1 format; task types and word counts per Teil at A2-C1; criteria names and point values for Schreiben/Sprechen at A2-C1; word-list sizes for A2 and B1 were seen in search excerpts, not in the opened PDF.
- telc: points, pass rules and task types for B1+ Beruf, B2, B2+ Beruf, C1, C1 Beruf; formats of A1, A2 and the dual-level Beruf exams (B1·B2 Beruf, B2·C1 Beruf); whether the 2020 B1 Übungstest is still the current format (A2·B1 from 2023 uses another scheme).
- DTB: totals and pass rule at A2, B1, C1; written times for B1 and C1; duration of the oral exam at A2, B1, C1; whether an edition newer than 2020/2021 changed the B2 rule.
- DTZ and Leben in Deutschland formats.
- DaZ in Hessen schools (Intensivklassen, Intensivkurse, grading during language acquisition): VOGSV §§ 45-57, status in the base skill's `sources.md`; no Hessen curriculum for DaZ was read: check: kultus.hessen.de → Deutsch als Zweitsprache.
- Labour-law facts used in Beruf texts (Krankmeldung, Kündigungsfristen).
- Working ranges (text length, new words per lesson, timing per item) and the AFB mapping are our own defaults.

## How to re-check
- Open only: goethe.de, telc.net, bamf.de, coe.int / rm.coe.int, kultus.hessen.de, rv.hessenrecht.hessen.de. Send no project, course or learner details in a search.
- goethe.de and coe.int block some automated requests; the PDFs under `goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/` open directly. The free Übungstests (shop.telc.net, bamf.de) carry points, pass rule and criteria on the pages `Punkte und Gewichtung`.
- When a `check:` is resolved, change the line in the reference, move the entry into the table above, and note document date and section.
- Not sources: private course sites, forums, textbook publishers' summaries, exam-prep blogs.
