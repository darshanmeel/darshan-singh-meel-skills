## Table for the independent answers (goes into the report as "Nachgeprüft")
```markdown
| Task | Own answer (Probe, count or line reference) | Material says | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| AB 1b | Im Garten \| pflückt \| Oma \| rote Äpfel - Umstellprobe: Rote Äpfel pflückt Oma im Garten | same, 4 Satzglieder | yes |
| Test 1b | 3 × 4 verses; abab cdcd efef; V. 1/3 and 9/11 impure; 3 stresses, iambic; cadences f m f m | same | yes |
```
- Grammar: write the Probe you used, not only the result.
- Poems: write the rhyme letters and the stress pattern of every verse before reading the Erwartungshorizont.
- Open tasks: list the key points you expect first, then compare; a missing key point, or one the text does not support, is a finding.
- Points: count the answer slots of every part (gaps, commas to set, words to mark - not the letters a, b, c; one lettered item can hold two slots); points per slot × slots = part points, the same number in test and Erwartungshorizont. Then add the part points per task, the tasks to the total, the AFB shares to 100 %, and recompute `threshold = percent × total` for every row of the key.

## Frequent defects (look for these first)
- Lösung of an Umstellprobe lists the original sentence among the rearrangements, or omits a correct one.
- "Das Verb steht an zweiter Stelle" without saying that the second Satzglied is meant, beside an example where the verb is the fourth word.
- Sentence with a separable verb or a Prädikativ in a first Satzglieder exercise.
- Word with two allowed spellings as a dictation or gap item; `ss/ß` wrong in the material itself (`Strasse`, `heisst`).
- Missing comma before `dass`, `weil`, `ob` in instructions; straight quotes instead of „ “.
- Poem quoted from memory with one changed word, or a printed word "corrected" from memory by the reviewer (song text instead of poem text); verse numbers that shift in print; source line without death year.
- Stilmittel named for a line that does not show it (Alliteration across verses, Metapher for a Vergleich with `wie`).
- Metre stated for the whole poem although single verses deviate, and the deviation is demanded instead of "möglich".
- Erwartungshorizont with points for Darstellung in the Oberstufe; BE total includes the Darstellungsleistung; key with 6 rows instead of 15-0.
- Fehlerindex rounded (3,38 → 3 is right; 5,9 → 6 is wrong).
- English line under the example sentence or under the model answer; English glossary of the poem's vocabulary.
- Grundschule worksheet that prints on four pages or splits a task over two; sentences to be marked set in a narrow table column; answer lines too short for a full sentence.
- Animation: lower-case word at the start of a rearranged sentence; full stop that travels with a Satzglied.

## Verdict
Base rule. Deutsch addition: one Must in D2, D3 or D5 in a student-facing file → at best "Einsatzbereit nach Must-Korrekturen"; the same for "Wortlaut ungeprüft" (D4); a protected text printed (D4), a wrong key or weighting (D7), or more than three Must → "Überarbeiten".
