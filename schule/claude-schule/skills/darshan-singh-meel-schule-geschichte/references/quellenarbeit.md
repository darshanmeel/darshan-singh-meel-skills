# Geschichte: source work, judgement, dates, timelines, animations

Subject practice for writing and checking tasks. School conventions, not KC wording, unless a KC code is given.

## Terms that must be used right
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Quelle | testimony from the time under study (text, picture, object, sound) |
| Darstellung | later account about the past (historian, textbook, journalist, the task author) |
| Überrest · Tradition | left without intent to inform posterity (Akte, Rechnung, Gesetz, Gegenstand) · made to hand down a view (Chronik, Memoiren, Denkmal, Festrede) - KCGO A2 |
| Primärquelle · Sekundärquelle | first-hand testimony · testimony relying on others |
| Perspektivität | the standpoint of the author (role, interest, knowledge, addressee) shapes the source - KCGO A5 |
| Sachurteil · Werturteil | judgement by criteria and standards of the time · judgement by today's stated values |
| Anlass · Ursache · Folge | trigger · deeper causes (several, structural) · short- and long-term consequences |

## Analysis steps per material (order of sub-tasks, AFB I → III)
| Material | Formal (I) | Content (I-II) | Context and intention (II) | Judgement (III) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Textquelle | Gattung, Verfasser (role), Datum, Ort, Adressat, Anlass | Thema, Kernaussagen in Sinnabschnitten, with line references | historischer Kontext, Standpunkt, Wirkungsabsicht, sprachliche Mittel | Aussagewert/Glaubwürdigkeit; Sachurteil on the position; Werturteil when asked |
| Karikatur | Zeichner, Titel, Medium, Datum | describe: figures, objects, text, composition - no meaning yet | decode symbols and persons, name the event, state the message and the target | fairness, effect, what it leaves out; own position |
| Bildquelle (Gemälde, Foto, Plakat) | Urheber, Auftraggeber, Datum, Technik, Ort | describe from foreground to background | staging, symbols, purpose (propaganda, memory, document) | what the picture shows vs what happened |
| Statistik / Diagramm | Thema, Einheit, Zeitraum, Raum, Herkunft der Zahlen | highest/lowest values, trends, breaks - with numbers | link to events; what was counted how, by whom | limits (gaps, definitions, interests of the collector) |
| Karte (Geschichtskarte) | Thema, Raum, Zeit(en), Legende | describe state and change | explain with events | what the map simplifies or suggests (borders, colours) |
| Darstellung | Autor, Art (Fachbuch, Essay, Schulbuch), Jahr | These, Argumente, Belege | position in a controversy, Geschichtsbild (LK: A7) | Überzeugungskraft; comparison with sources or a second Darstellung |
| Zeitzeugenbericht (later) | who, when told, distance to the event | what is reported | memory vs event; later knowledge | value as source for the event vs for the memory |

- Info box of a source worksheet names these steps in at most 4 lines.
- A comparison task names the criteria or asks for them explicitly (U7; LK U10: justify the choice).
- Line references: number lines in fives when a task asks for evidence; solutions cite `Z. 4-6`.

## Sources: what may be printed, how it is cited
| Kind | Rule |
|---|---|
| Official texts (laws, constitutions, treaties, decrees, proclamations, court decisions) | free to print; cite title, date, article/paragraph, "zitiert nach" the official or archival page. check: UrhG § 5 (gesetze-im-internet.de) |
| Author dead for more than 70 years | public domain in Germany; later translations and edited versions have their own protection - use the original or an old translation and say which. check: UrhG § 64 |
| Newer texts, textbook texts, Abitur materials, press photos, modern cartoons | not copied. Describe in own words (cartoon, photo), summarise as Darstellung with the reference, or let the user supply a licensed copy. Quoting short passages for analysis: check: UrhG § 51 and § 60a - the skill does not rely on them |
| Pictures | no embedded scans. Own schematic SVG, or a description in words: "Bildbeschreibung (verfasst für diese Aufgabe) nach: <Urheber, Titel, Jahr, Sammlung>" |
| Written for the task | labelled "Darstellung, verfasst für diese Aufgabe / account written for this task". Fictional voices only as clearly marked fiction ("erfundener Tagebucheintrag / invented diary entry") and never for real persons, never for victims or perpetrators of mass crimes |

Citation line under every source (German, with English line):
`<Verfasser/Institution>: <Titel oder Gattung>, <Datum>, <Ort>. Zitiert nach: <Edition oder amtliche Seite, Abrufjahr>. Gekürzt [...] · Rechtschreibung originalgetreu | modernisiert. Gemeinfrei | amtliches Werk.`

- Quotations are exact: wording, paragraph number, punctuation. Cuts `[...]`, additions `[ ]`. Old spelling is kept (`Erlaubniß`) or the modernisation is stated.
- Excerpts must not turn the meaning: read the sentences before and after the cut.
- Translations of foreign sources: cite the translator/edition; own translation is labelled "eigene Übersetzung" and kept close.

## Judgement tasks
- The task names the kind: `Beurteilen Sie ...` (Sachurteil) · `Bewerten Sie ... / Nehmen Sie Stellung ...` (Werturteil, own values stated) · `Erörtern Sie ...` (pro and contra, then a conclusion).
- Solution/Erwartungshorizont: criteria (e.g. Legitimität, Effektivität, Handlungsspielraum, Folgen, Reichweite, Dauer), arguments for both directions, one model answer, the sentence "andere begründete Urteile sind gleichwertig / other reasoned judgements are of equal value".
- Points for judgement tasks go to criteria, evidence from the material, and coherence - never to one opinion.
- Present-day links (O3) compare by named criteria; no equating of past and present.

## Dates and facts: checking rule
- Per date note what it marks: beschlossen, unterzeichnet, verkündet, in Kraft, Beginn, Ende. Give the day only when the source gives it; otherwise month or year.
- Check against at least one official or scholarly public page and name it under the material: Deutscher Bundestag (bundestag.de, Parlamentsgeschichte, Textarchiv), Bundesarchiv (bundesarchiv.de), Deutsches Historisches Museum - LeMO (dhm.de/lemo), Bundeszentrale für politische Bildung (bpb.de), Haus der Geschichte (hdg.de), Arolsen Archives, Yad Vashem, gesetze-im-internet.de, documentarchiv of state archives.
- Sources differ (typical: day of the vote, date of the law, day of promulgation): use the date all pages share and name the act it marks ("verkündet am 28. März 1849").
- Old-style/new-style calendars (Russia before 1918: Februar-/Oktoberrevolution) : give both or say which.
- Numbers of victims, populations, votes: with source and the word "etwa" where research gives ranges; no single precise figure for estimates.
- Names: full name on first use with office and years where needed (`Friedrich Wilhelm IV., König von Preußen 1840-1861`).

## Typical student errors (use for "Typischer Fehler" lines)
- Source retold or copied instead of summarised; judgement inside the summary.
- Author's opinion taken as fact; Darstellung treated as source.
- Anlass taken as Ursache; one cause for a complex event.
- Anachronism: today's values or knowledge assumed for the actors ("they should have known ...").
- Date of proclamation taken as entry into force; signing as ratification.
- Cartoon: interpretation before description; symbols not decoded.
- Statistics: absolute numbers and shares mixed; base year ignored.
- Judgement = opinion without criteria and without reference to the material.
- Epoch borders taken as real breaks for the people of the time.

## Timelines
- `scripts/zeitleiste.py START ENDE events.txt` - equal time, equal length; ticks chosen by span (months, years, decades, centuries) and set on whole steps (first of a month, 1 January). Events as `DATE|Beschriftung DE / EN`; one date per event - a second day goes into the caption or solution.
- Never draw or move marks by hand. Caption: "maßstabsgetreu", START and ENDE as passed to the script, source of the dates.
- `--anim` gives the stage for the base `animation.html`: `id="buehne"`, events hidden, progress line `#lauf`.
- Not more than 8 events per line of the axis; long spans plus one dense phase → two timelines (overview and detail), not a distorted one. A broken axis is marked with `//` and named in the caption.
- Caption states: "maßstabsgetreu / true to scale" and the source of the dates.
- Parallel developments (two states, politics/economy): one axis, two labelled bands above and below.
- Before Christ: negative years in the script; labels `v. Chr.`; no year 0.

## Animations: when and what
Use the base template; one idea per step; every date equals worksheet and solution.

| Content | Steps (4-8) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Timeline of a process (revolution, crisis, road to war, unification) | empty axis → one event per step, a bar grows along the axis | x positions from `zeitleiste.py` |
| Phases of a revolution or regime change | one phase per step: actors, institution, turning point | name the phase model as a construct |
| Territorial change (schematic) | borders before → change → after; one change per step | own schematic sketch, "stark vereinfacht"; no copied map |
| Migration, flight, trade routes | origin → routes in order → destinations → numbers with source | flows simplified; year per step |
| Constitution scheme (who elects whom) | organs appear → arrows one by one | arrows labelled (wählt, ernennt, kontrolliert) |
| Cause-effect chain | Ursachen → Anlass → Ereignis → Folgen | several causes side by side, not one line |
| Reading a cartoon | picture → elements highlighted one by one → message | only with an own drawing or a public-domain cartoon |

Not an animation: a single source, a portrait, a finished scheme - use a figure in `erklaerung.html`. No animated violence, no dramatising effects.

## Bili Geschichte (teaching language English)
- Default of these skills stays: German first, English line under it; sources German with word list. For a class taught in English the user chooses "zusätzlich ganz englische Fassung" (`.en.html`); English-language sources are then printed in the original with a German word list.
- Operators: official DE ↔ EN pairs of the Hessen list for bilingual subjects (base `operatoren.md`). German history terms without an English equivalent stay German in italics with a gloss (`Reichstag`, `Kaiser`, `Gleichschaltung`): `begriffe.md`.
- Rules for bilingual classes: check: Hessen regulations on bilingual teaching (kultus.hessen.de) and the school's concept.
