## Not verified
- Usual placement of topics by year in Grundschule (`grundschule.md`) and inside the double years of Sek I: planning help from practice; the Schulcurriculum / Fachcurriculum decides.
- Written subtraction method and division notation in Grundschule: school decision.
- When the calculator is introduced in Sek I; whether Klassenarbeiten have a hilfsmittelfreier Teil: school decision.
- GTR (graphing calculator without CAS): not named in the Abiturerlass 2027. check: Abiturerlass of the exam year.
- Lists of typical mistakes, number-choice rules, animation list: didactic practice, no official source.
- English terms in `begriffe.md`: own selection of standard British school terms; no official Hessen list for bilingual Mathematik was read.
- Sek I and Grundschule operator lists and percentage grade key: none statewide (base `sources.md`). Rounding Sek I thresholds up to the next half point (`konventionen.md`): own convention; the Fachkonferenz decides.
- Whether the KC Sek I of 2011 is still the current edition: check: kultus.hessen.de → Kerncurricula → Sekundarstufe I.
- KMK Bildungsstandards Primarbereich and Mittlerer Schulabschluss (revised 2022): not read; the Hessen KC of 2011 is the binding text used here.
