# Deutsch gymnasiale Oberstufe (E, Q1-Q4) and Landesabitur, Hessen

Sources: KCGO Deutsch, Ausgabe 2024, Stand 01.08.2025; Abiturerlass Landesabitur 2027 (2 July 2025, changed 2 Feb 2026) and 2028 (28 May 2026). Status: `sources.md`. - Qualifikationsphase: nine binding Themenfelder for the written Abitur - three in two of the half-years Q1-Q3, two in the third, one in Q4; set by Erlass.
- Binding Themenfelder take about two thirds of the teaching time (about 12 weeks per half-year); the rest deepens them or takes an optional Themenfeld.
- "zum Beispiel" = suggestion; "oder" = the teacher chooses (Ganzschrift or shorter texts).
- Content bullets per Themenfeld, split into grundlegendes and erhöhtes Niveau: KCGO section 3.3. GK material never needs erhöhtes Niveau.

## Klausuren in the course
- Number, length (as a rule 90 minutes; one Klausur under Abitur conditions), weights: base `bewertung.md`.
- Task set like the Abitur: material (text with line numbers, source line) + 2-3 tasks with Landesabitur operators, Sie-form, all three AFB. Working default for the shares: base `bewertung.md`.
- Points: Bewertungseinheiten (BE) for the Verstehensleistung only; Darstellungsleistung is judged as a whole in points (0-15). Combination 70 : 30 and Fehlerindex: `schreibaufgaben.md`.
- 90 minutes: one short text (poem up to about 20 verses, prose or Sachtext up to about 450 words; own estimate) and at most three tasks. Abitur-length tasks only for the Klausur under Abitur conditions.
- LK: add a task part that needs erhöhtes Niveau (programmatic text, theory, comparison); GK: none.
- Students write on Klausurbögen; the test file carries material, tasks and the assessment box, no answer lines.
