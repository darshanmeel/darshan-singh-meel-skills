## Jg. 1/2 (KC text, summarised)
- Schriftspracherwerb is the centre: Lautprinzip, Laut-Buchstaben-Zuordnung, position of a sound in the word.
- Druckschrift first, then a handwriting others can read.
- Structures: Laut, Buchstabe, Alphabet, Wortbausteine, Wörter, Wortarten.
- Writing "um sich auszudrücken" first; spelling rules and strategies built up step by step.
- Reading: technical reading first (Sichtwortschatz, recognising word groups), then the link between sections and the whole text.

## Language level of the material
- Sentences up to about 10 words, one action per sentence, du-form, present tense in instructions.
- One instruction per line; the operator is the first word after an optional lead-in sentence.
- Same word for the same thing: `Nomen` everywhere or `Namenwort` everywhere (Jg. 1-2 often Namenwort, Tunwort, Wiewort - ask).
- `<body class="gs">`; example words in the info box, at most 4 lines.
- English help line: one short line per instruction; none on words, sentences and texts the child reads, writes or sorts.
- Print: 14 pt and 12 mm lines fill a page fast - one task per page at most, a worksheet for one lesson at most 3 pages; material, question and answer lines of a task sit in one `.teil` so that they do not split; glossary 3 terms with one-line meanings.
- Sentences in which the child draws lines or circles: `style="word-spacing:.7em"`, never in a narrow table column.
- Verb position: say "das zweite Satzglied", not "die zweite Stelle" (children count words).

## Typical mistakes to build into Lösung and Tipps
| Topic | Mistake | Proof to teach |
|---|---|---|
| Großschreibung | Nomen klein, Satzanfang klein | Artikelprobe (der/die/das davor), Mehrzahl bilden |
| Auslautverhärtung | Hunt, Berk, gelp | Verlängern: Hunde, Berge, gelbe |
| ä/äu vs. e/eu | Beume, Hende | Ableiten: Baum, Hand |
| Doppelkonsonant | komen, Bal | kurzer Vokal, Silben schwingen: kom-men |
| ie | Bine, spilen | langes i meist ie |
| Satzglieder | every word counted as a Satzglied; verb moved from second place | Umstellprobe: what moves together is one Satzglied |
| Wortarten | Adjektiv vs. Verb | Steigerung (schnell, schneller) vs. Personalform (ich laufe, du läufst) |
| wörtliche Rede | Zeichen fehlen, Doppelpunkt fehlt | Begleitsatz markieren |
