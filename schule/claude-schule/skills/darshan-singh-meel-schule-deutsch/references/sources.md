## Not verified (carried as `check:` in the references)
- VOGSV Anlage 2 Nr. 2.3: thresholds and exact deduction of the Fehlerindex in Jg. 9/10 (only the summary on the Maßnahmenpaket page was read).
- Diktat error keys and percentage keys for Sek I and Grundschule: none statewide found; Fachkonferenz.
- Current edition of the amtliches Regelwerk (rechtschreibrat.com): not opened.
- Text lengths, dictation word counts, placement of topics by year, the Sek I weighting Inhalt/Darstellung, the wording of the Darstellungsleistung grid, the Deutsch work verbs for Grundschule and Jg. 5-6: working defaults of this skill, no official source.
- English grammar and literary terms: own selection of standard school terms; no official bilingual list for Deutsch.
- Wording of public-domain texts in `examples/` (Eichendorff, Mondnacht): written from knowledge, spelling modernised; no edition or official page was used. V. 4 is printed as `Von ihm nun träumen müsst’`; the variant `nur` circulates through the song text. The example stays "not ready: Wortlaut" until compared with the class edition.
- UrhG § 64 (70 years after the author's death): general rule stated from knowledge, not legal advice.

## How to re-check
Open only the official pages above. Send no school, class or student details in a search. When a `check:` is resolved, change the line in the reference and move the entry into the table with document date and section. A new Abiturerlass (issued about two years before the exam): add a column or row in `oberstufe.md`, keep the older year until its exam is over.
