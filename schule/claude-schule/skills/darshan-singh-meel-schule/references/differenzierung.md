# Differentiation and inclusion

Legal lines: VOGSV (Hessen); status in `sources.md`. Didactic lines are working rules, not law.

## Three levels (default in every worksheet)
| Level | Mark | AFB | How the task differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basis | ★ | I | one step, familiar numbers/material, worked example or sentence starter beside it |
| Standard | ★★ | I-II | the lesson goal as stated; no extra help |
| Herausforderung | ★★★ | II-III | open or reversed question, own reasoning, transfer to a new case - not "more of the same" |

- Same topic and same goal on all levels; the level changes help and complexity, not the content.
- Students choose the level; the plan says who is steered where and what fast finishers do.
- Help comes as Tippkarten (`Tipp 1`, `Tipp 2`) or a Wortspeicher, not as a different worksheet per student.
- Graded tests are not levelled by choice: same test for all; differentiation there only through Nachteilsausgleich.

## DaZ (German as a second language)
- Wortspeicher on the sheet: nouns with article and plural, verbs with an example sentence; 6-10 words.
- Sentence starters for AFB II/III answers (`Die Pflanze wächst nicht, weil ...`).
- Short main clauses; one instruction per sentence; no passive chains; the same word for the same thing throughout.
- Pictures, tables and arrows carry the content beside the text.
- The English line is not a DaZ aid by itself - use it only where the student reads English.
- Law: students of non-German home language, support courses and grading during language acquisition: VOGSV §§ 45-57 (headings read; details: check: §§ 50-57).

## LRS (reading and spelling difficulties), Rechenschwäche
- Layout: sans-serif, 12-14 pt, line height 1.5, left-aligned, no italics or capitals for running text, short lines, one task per block, generous answer space.
- Read tasks aloud or supply audio; allow oral answers; mark content, not spelling, where the support plan says so.
- Maths: squared answer areas, steps one below the other, a worked example, concrete material named in the plan.
- Law: VOGSV §§ 37-44 (special difficulties in reading, spelling, calculating); § 42 Nachteilsausgleich and deviations in assessment; individual Förderplan (§ 40).

## Nachteilsausgleich (VOGSV § 7; for LRS/Rechnen § 42)
| Type | Meaning | Examples in the text of § 7 | In reports |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nachteilsausgleich | same demands, different conditions | more time; technical aids; larger print, specially designed worksheets; own workplace, breaks; differentiated homework | not noted |
| Abweichen bei der Leistungsfeststellung | same subject demands, different task form | differentiated task; oral instead of written | not noted |
| Abweichen bei der Leistungsbewertung (Notenschutz) | lower subject demands | differentiated task; stronger weight on oral work; spelling not marked for a time | check: § 7 Abs. 4-5 and § 42 (note in the report; final exams unaffected) |

- Material offers it; the Klassenkonferenz/school decides it. Never write a student's name or diagnosis into material; write `Fassung mit Nachteilsausgleich` and what differs (time, print size, aids).
- Large-print version: same file, `<body class="gs">` (bigger type, wider lines).

## Sonderpädagogischer Förderbedarf
- Zielgleich (same curriculum): Nachteilsausgleich as above.
- Zieldifferent (e.g. Förderschwerpunkt Lernen): own goals from the individual Förderplan; material states the reduced goal; grading follows the Förderplan - check: VOSB (Verordnung über Unterricht, Erziehung und sonderpädagogische Förderung) for the rules; not read.
- Material: fewer tasks, same topic; concrete → pictorial → symbolic; one new step per task.

## High achievers
- Herausforderung tasks first, not after all others; open problems, proofs, own examples, explaining to a partner; no extra homework as "reward".
