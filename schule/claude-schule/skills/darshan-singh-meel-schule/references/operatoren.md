# Operators and Anforderungsbereiche (AFB)

Sources and status: `sources.md`. Operator names and AFB placement below: Hessen Landesabitur 2027 operator lists (science list: Stand 1 August 2026; Deutsch/FB II list and bilingual list: Stand 1 August 2025). The lists are reissued per exam year: check: the list for the course's exam year before a Klausur.

## Anforderungsbereiche
| AFB | Core | Typical task |
|---|---|---|
| I | reproduce: facts, terms, methods in the context they were learned | name, describe, calculate with a practised method |
| II | reorganise and transfer: select, order, explain known content; apply it to a comparable new case | explain, compare, analyse, apply |
| III | reflect and solve problems: handle new, complex cases; reach own conclusions, justifications, judgements | assess, discuss, prove, develop |

- AFB are a property of tasks, not of competences (KCGO; OAVO § 25 Abs. 4). Exact wording of the definitions: check: OAVO § 25 Abs. 4.
- An operator has a main AFB; the real AFB depends on the task and on what was taught. A practised routine is AFB I whatever the verb.
- Mix in a test: `bewertung.md`.

## Rules for task wording
- One operator per task or sub-task, as the first word. Du-form in Grundschule and Sek I (`Berechne`), Sie-form in the Oberstufe (`Berechnen Sie`).
- Only operators the class has practised. No "Was ist ...?" or "Kannst du ...?" in graded work.
- State material, scope and form: what to use, how many, in what form (`in ganzen Sätzen`, `mit Rechenweg`, `in einer Tabelle`).
- Operators of AFB II and III need a reference to text or material and expect reasons.
- English line: the operator from the EN column, same position, same imperative (`Calculate ...`). Never a softer verb.

## Sek I and Grundschule
No state operator list exists for Sek I or Grundschule (none found on the pages in `sources.md`). Working default, to be replaced by the school's Fachcurriculum list:

| Level | Operators (du-form) |
|---|---|
| Grundschule | Nenne · Zeige · Male / Zeichne · Ordne · Rechne / Berechne · Schreibe auf · Beschreibe · Erkläre · Vergleiche · Begründe (from Jg. 3) |
| Jg. 5-6 | Nenne · Beschreibe · Berechne · Zeichne · Ordne zu · Erkläre · Vergleiche · Begründe |
| Jg. 7-10 | the Oberstufe list of the subject, du-form; add Erläutere, Analysiere, Werte aus, Beurteile, Bewerte step by step |

- Grundschule: one action per sentence; the operator may follow a short lead-in (`Schau dir das Bild an. Beschreibe ...`).
- Introduce each operator with a model answer before it appears in graded work.
