# Grading and written work (Hessen)

Legal basis: VOGSV (Sek I, Grundschule), OAVO (Oberstufe). Status per line: verified in the text named in `sources.md`, or `check:`. Rules change - for anything a grade depends on, tell the user which version was used.

## Sek I and Grundschule: grade key
- No statewide percentage key was found in the VOGSV. The key is set by the Fachkonferenz / school. **Ask for it**; if unknown use the default and print "Vorschlag - an den Beschluss der Fachkonferenz anpassen" under it.
- Default (same thresholds as the Oberstufe table, grouped by Note): 1 ab 85 % · 2 ab 70 % · 3 ab 55 % · 4 ab 40 % · 5 ab 20 % · 6 darunter. Many schools set "ausreichend" at 45 % or 50 % - one question settles it.
- Thresholds in whole or half points; every grade reachable; no gaps or overlaps.

## Procedure rules that shape a test
- Announce content frame and date at least 5 school days ahead (VOGSV § 33).
- Per student: one written paper per day, max. three per week (Grundschule: two) (VOGSV § 28). Oberstufe: as a rule not more than three Leistungsnachweise per week.
- Only content that was taught and practised; the Oberstufe Klausur covers a fair number of Themenfelder of the half-year and uses the Anforderungsbereiche.
- Return within three school weeks as a rule, with a Notenspiegel under each paper (not applied in the Oberstufe as far as § 33 Abs. 2 goes: check: OAVO § 9).
- More than one third mangelhaft/ungenügend → repeat once unless the head teacher rules it counts; more than half → must be repeated; the better grade counts; new tasks from the same unit (VOGSV § 34, Anlage 2 Nr. 4). Oberstufe: OAVO rules apply instead - check: OAVO § 9.
- Weight: in subjects with Klassenarbeiten written work is half of the grade basis, in other subjects about a third (VOGSV § 32 Abs. 3). Oberstufe: where one Klausur per half-year is written it counts about a third; classwork counts at least as much as the Leistungsnachweise (OAVO § 9 Abs. 2).

## Language and form in the mark
| Level | Rule |
|---|---|
| Jg. 5-8 | formal errors (grammar, spelling, punctuation) count in every subject but lower the grade by a limited step only - wording and size: check: VOGSV Anlage 2 Nr. 2.2 |
| Jg. 9-10 | serious, frequent errors count in every subject; texts of 100+ words use an error index - check: Anlage 2 Nr. 2.3 |
| Oberstufe, most subjects | deduction of 1 or 2 points for serious or frequent language/form errors (OAVO § 9 Abs. 12, Anlage 9b I) |
| Oberstufe Deutsch | Verstehensleistung : Darstellungsleistung = 70 : 30 (OAVO § 9 Abs. 12a) |
| Oberstufe moderne Fremdsprachen | Sprache : Inhalt = 60 : 40 (§ 9 Abs. 13; check: current Erlass) |

## AFB mix in a test
- Official: Oberstufe tasks cover all three Anforderungsbereiche and prepare for the Abitur (OAVO § 9 Abs. 3, § 25 Abs. 4). Shares: check: OAVO § 25 Abs. 4 and the subject's KMK standards.
- Working default (not official): AFB II is the largest share, AFB I larger than AFB III; Sek I about 30-40 % I, 40-50 % II, 10-20 % III; Oberstufe about 30 % I, 40-50 % II, 20-30 % III. Grundschule: mostly I and II, one III task.
- "Ausreichend" must be reachable with AFB I plus easy AFB II tasks.

## Erwartungshorizont rules
- Points per sub-task, split into the steps that earn them (e.g. Ansatz 1, Rechnung 2, Antwortsatz 1).
- Sum of sub-tasks = total; total on the test = total on the Erwartungshorizont; AFB table adds up to the total.
- Folgefehler: a correct continuation with a wrong earlier result earns the later points.
- Other correct ways: full points. State it.
- Every entry has its English line like all other material (`bilingual.md`); points, AFB and the grade key are language-neutral and stand once.
