## How to re-check
- Open only the official pages above (kultus.hessen.de, rv.hessenrecht.hessen.de, verkuendung.hessen.de, kmk.org). Send no project, school or student details in a search.
- When a `check:` is resolved, change the line in the reference, move the entry up into the table, and note document date and paragraph.
- Not sources: private school sites, forums, grade calculators, textbooks.
