## Common defects found in Geschichte material
- Verkündung taken as Inkrafttreten; "trat nie in Kraft" for a text that was valid in part of the states.
- Article quoted in today's wording under a historical date.
- Source excerpt without paragraph numbers, or numbering taken from a secondary synopsis without saying so.
- Summary solution that judges; judgement solution with one accepted opinion.
- Timeline with equal gaps between unequal intervals.
- English line translates the source text instead of leaving it German.
- "Die Deutschen", "die Franzosen" as actors; single-cause chains.
- NS vocabulary without quotation marks in info boxes.
